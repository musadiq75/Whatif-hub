# VIDEO 1 — ALL 43 SHOT PROMPTS (production-ready)

**How to use:** generate the KEYFRAME image first (prompt = Keyframe block), then run image-to-video with the Motion prompt. Every prompt already contains the full continuity payload. Durations are pacing targets; re-time against the real narration audio (see runbook step 2).

**STYLE CORE (baked into every prompt below):** cinematic photorealism with subtle 3D-render polish, 35mm anamorphic character, 2.39:1 letterbox, teal-shifted shadows, warm sodium-amber highlights, gentle film grain, volumetric light, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, protagonist's face never visible.

**CONTINUITY TOKENS (global, apply to every shot):**
- MAN: male, 30s, medium build, dark short hair, light stubble; faceless (rear/3-quarter rear/silhouette only)
- WARDROBE: heather-gray tee + sleep shorts (Scenes 1–2), dark jeans + olive jacket (Scenes 3+)
- BEDROOM: bed left of window; amber digital clock right nightstand (11:59→12:00 in S1, 7:02 in 6-6); loaded wooden bookshelf above dresser opposite bed; five-blade dark-wood ceiling fan
- STREET: same suburban corner, lamp post right, hedge line left
- BRIDGE-OLD: green riveted truss, rust streaks; BRIDGE-NEW: gray cable-stayed, tall pylons
- AIRLINER: generic white twin-engine narrow-body; SPARROW: brown house sparrow
- CITY: mid-rise US riverfront skyline
